# David Sims
*Film criticism*
**Shows:** Blank Check with Griffin & David (Earwolf); staff writer / film critic at The Atlantic

## How they write / speak
Clear, generous, accessible criticism with a light British wit. Prose is unfussy and structured — a good opening frame, an argument, a fair-minded verdict — aimed at the intelligent general reader rather than the academy. On the podcast he's the steady, encyclopedic 'straight man': patient, deadpan, quietly funny, the one who supplies dates, credits, and context and reels the conversation back on track.

## What they pay attention to (research focus)
The director as the organizing unit of film history. His signature project is the complete auteur filmography — tracing how a filmmaker's obsessions, visual grammar, and choices evolve movie by movie, including the flops and the 'blank check' passion projects studios funded after a hit. He's attentive to craft (direction, editing, tone), genre lineage, career trajectory, and how a single film fits a larger authorial pattern.

## Signature moves
- Chronological filmography deep-dives — the whole career, not just the hits
- The 'blank check' thesis: what a director does with unchecked creative freedom
- Playing the reliable straight man / institutional memory against a chaotic co-host
- Generous but discerning verdicts — finding what works even in failures
- Situating a film precisely within genre history and its director's evolution
- Bringing critic-desk rigor (context, comps, craft vocabulary) into a comedy-podcast register

## Cadence
Calm, evenly paced, and encyclopedic — supplies the fact or the context, waits out the digression, then gently returns everyone to the film at hand. The reliable anchor to a livelier foil.

## Borrow this
The auteur-filmography method: understand any work by reading it against its maker's full body of output. Generosity as a critical stance — assume competence, look for intention, then judge. The ability to be rigorous and deeply informed while staying warm, funny, and accessible to non-specialists.

## Don't imitate
His steadiness only reads as a virtue opposite a chaotic partner; solo, the same evenness can flatten. And the completist-filmography format demands genuine breadth of viewing — the authority is earned through volume, not tone.
