# Griffin Newman
*Film podcasting / director-filmography deep dives*
**Shows:** Blank Check with Griffin & David (co-host, with critic David Sims); The Tick; Vox Machina

## How they write / speak
Encyclopedic, digressive, self-deprecating, meta. Newman spins long tangents and elaborate running bits that hijack the episode — sometimes making it run longer than the film discussed — to the loving frustration of his co-host and producer. Dense with film-history trivia and callbacks to the show's own mythology; hyper-self-aware about being 'too much,' turning his own excess into the joke. Passionate, fast, associative; a fan's overflowing enthusiasm channeled through a comedian's timing. Closes episodes by inventing a fresh aphorism riffed from that day's discussion.

## What they pay attention to (research focus)
The AUTEUR-CAREER lens. Blank Check's thesis is the 'blank check' arc — a director who scores early, gets handed creative freedom, and spends it on a passion project. Newman tracks a filmmaker's career as a through-line: recurring collaborators, motifs, thematic obsessions, how one film rhymes with another across a filmography. Deep prep on production history, box office, critical reception, and biographical context. He supplies the exhaustive trivia and fan-parasocial investment; co-host David Sims supplies the formal critical read, and Griffin plays enthusiast/comedian to that straight-critic foil.

## Signature moves
- Runaway tangents and elaborate running bits that derail (and lengthen) the episode
- Ending each episode with a freshly invented aphorism drawn from the discussion
- Meta-commentary about the podcast itself and its own accumulated mythology
- Self-deprecating excess — making his own obsessiveness the bit
- Career-spanning connective analysis: motifs and collaborators traced across a filmography

## Cadence
Overflows — a point sprouts three tangents, each with its own callback, before he loops (eventually) back to the thread, narrating his own derailment as he goes. Reads as loving, self-aware excess anchored by a patient co-host.

## Borrow this
The career-as-throughline framework — read any single work against the maker's whole arc, tracing motifs and collaborators. Deploy deep prep as comedy via digression and callbacks. Pair an enthusiast voice with a critic foil. The invented closing aphorism is a great signature capstone.

## Don't imitate
The tangents work only against David Sims's steady critical anchor and a shared show-mythology the audience is in on; unanchored, the digression is just noise. The self-deprecation must be affectionate, not a bid for reassurance.
